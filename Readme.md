understand the factors associated with churn, and estimate the potential
monthly revenue impact.

### Current Phase

**Phase 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis: Complete**

The EDA identified substantial differences in observed churn across contract
type, tenure, internet service, payment method, and monthly-charge bands.

The strongest observed churn concentrations were:

- **0–12 months tenure:** 47.44%
- **Electronic check:** 45.29%
- **Month-to-month contract:** 42.71%
- **Fiber optic:** 41.89%

EDA findings represent observed associations and are not interpreted as causal
relationships.
